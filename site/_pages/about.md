I am a PhD candidate in financial economics at the University of Rennes (CREM, CNRS UMR 6211). My
industrial PhD (CIFRE) was carried out with Arkéa Asset Management,
where I have worked since 2021, first on the private wealth management desk and then in the
quantamental team. I will defend my dissertation, *Essays on Crypto-Asset Markets: Inefficiencies,
Portfolio Strategies, and Systemic Risk*, on **27 November 2026**.

Research interests
------

My research examines the economics of digital assets and its implications for quantitative asset
management:

- **Statistical arbitrage**: cointegration relationships between crypto-asset pairs and pair
  trading strategies with optimised trading thresholds.
- **Speculative bubbles**: detection with log-periodic power law (LPPLS) and mixed
  causal–noncausal models, and their link with systemic risk.
- **Connectedness and systemic risk** across crypto-asset market sectors and between crypto-assets
  and equities.
- **Machine learning** for portfolio construction and asset selection under uncertainty.

My work is published in [*Quantitative Finance*](/publications/) and has been presented at
[13 international conferences and seminars](/talks/), including the World Finance Conference and
the Forecasting Financial Markets Conference.
