**Abstract.** This paper examines alternative portfolio selection approaches in the context of the pronounced volatility and uncertainty characterising cryptocurrency markets. Uncertainty is defined as the erratic nature of performance and the limited reliability of active strategies in sustaining consistent behaviour. Such uncertainty is pernicious, as it often materialises in substantial capital losses when market timing risk emerges. In contrast to the pursuit of rapid speculative gains, this study adopts a framework centred on managing uncertainty through the optimisation of predictability; thereby mitigating exposure to recurrent waves of instability. In this respect, this exploration targets active investment strategies that converge towards a MAXNAV portfolio under the Maximally Machine Learnable Portfolio (MMLP). The MAXNAV rules are designed to preserve the investor's highest recorded net asset value. They embed a protective mechanism against downside risk while maintaining exposure to upside potential. The findings indicate that the equally weighted market portfolio possesses sufficient predictability to be exploited for long-term protective investment. Indeed, prevailing mean signals combined with the risk-averse utility principle provide robust protection by managing exposure across all market contexts.
